Common questions

Do I need to share my location? No. Search for a city instead. Without any place, plans use your schedule only.

Why did my plan move? SunSeek moves the suggested time around rain, heat, and high UV in the forecast, and around your schedule and routines.

How do I restore SunSeek Premium? Settings → SunSeek Premium → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel? iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → SunSeek Premium. Your data stays when Premium ends.

How do I export or delete my data? Settings → Your data. Both are free.
