What SunSeek is

SunSeek is a practical daylight planning companion. It suggests times to step outside based on your schedule, sunrise and sunset, and the weather forecast. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Forecasts can be wrong; use your own judgment and follow local official weather advice.

SunSeek Premium

The daily plan, weather alternatives, logging, widgets, reminders, journal, 7-day review, one routine, export, and data deletion are free. SunSeek Premium adds the week-ahead planner, more named routines, weekly adjustments, routine experiments, and 12-week trends. It is an auto-renewable subscription: $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year (US prices; other regions vary), each with a 1-week free trial for eligible new subscribers.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when a free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.
