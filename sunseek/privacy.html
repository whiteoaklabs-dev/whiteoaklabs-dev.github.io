SunSeek by White Oak Labs

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

SunSeek: Light & Sleep ("SunSeek") is made by White Oak Labs. It is a local-first daylight planner: no account, no backend, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics service. Nothing in the app requires a permission. A searched city works without location access, and Today's plan works without notifications or Apple Health.

The short version

White Oak Labs does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. What you enter stays on your device.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your schedule: wake and bed times, weekday and weekend free time, rest days, one-off changes, preferred time and length, and any named routines (such as Office or Home) with the weekdays that use them
  • Outdoor time you record (timer or manual sessions, with start and end times and any edits), check-ins, streaks, and optional sleep and energy ratings
  • The plans SunSeek showed you and your optional evening closeouts (done, moved, skipped, or forgot, with an optional reason or note)
  • The outdoor timer's state, so a running timer survives a relaunch. Sessions, plans, and the timer are kept in an App Group container on this device so the widget and Live Activity can show and record them
  • Sky journal photos. SunSeek keeps its own copy of each photo inside the app
  • Settings, accent, units, and reminder preferences
  • A short-lived forecast cache (one place, rounded to about 1 km, replaced on each refresh)
  • An optional diagnostics log, off by default. When on, it records event names only (no times, places, ratings, or Health data) in a small file on the device. It is never uploaded, and turning it off deletes it

None of this is sent to White Oak Labs, and SunSeek does not sync it to iCloud.

Export and delete

Settings → Your data exports your sessions, plans, closeouts, ratings, routines, and routine experiments as JSON and CSV files (optionally with your journal photos) through the share sheet. "Delete all SunSeek data" removes everything SunSeek stored on this device. Both are free and never need a subscription. Apple Health data is never changed. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Sent to Apple to answer a request

  • Weather. When a place is set, its coordinate (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to Apple WeatherKit to get the forecast and any weather alerts. Identical requests are answered from the on-device cache for 30 minutes.
  • City search. What you type in the city search goes to Apple Maps search. Choosing a place or using your location may look up the city name with Apple.

SunSeek does not send this information to White Oak Labs. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Location (optional)

SunSeek asks for location only when you tap "Use my location". The coordinate is stored on the device and shared with the SunSeek widget through an App Group on that same device. SunSeek does not track your location in the background. If you decline, search for a city instead.

Apple Health (optional, read-only)

If you connect Apple Health, SunSeek reads Sleep and Time in Daylight for the last 14 days. These reads stay on the device and are shown separately from the time you record in SunSeek. SunSeek never writes to Apple Health and never sends Health data anywhere. You can turn access off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Notifications (optional)

Reminders are off until you turn them on. They are scheduled locally on the device, not delivered through a push server.

Camera and photos (optional)

The camera or photo library is used only when you add a sky photo. SunSeek stores its own copy inside the app on this device.

Purchases (optional)

SunSeek Premium is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account; SunSeek never sees your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with StoreKit on this device and keeps a copy of that status (plan and renewal date) so it works offline. Nothing about purchases is sent to White Oak Labs. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Feedback

Feedback and support open an email you choose to send. We receive only what you write and use it only to reply.

Children

SunSeek is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Planning tool, not medical advice

SunSeek is a planning tool. It does not give medical advice, dosing (including vitamin D), or treatment for sleep or jet lag. Weather safety tips (UV protection, heat) are general guidance; follow local official advice.
