SunSeek by White Oak Labs

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

SunSeek: Outdoor Break Planner ("SunSeek") is made by White Oak Labs. It plans short outdoor breaks around your workday, your calendar's busy times and the weather forecast. There is no account, no backend, no ads, no tracking and no analytics service.

The short version

White Oak Labs does not collect, sell or share your personal information. What you enter stays on your device.

Stored only on your device

Your schedule and routines (including Office/Home buffers and favorite break spots), the plans SunSeek suggests, the outside time you log, check-ins and optional evening notes and energy ratings, sky journal photos, and your settings. The widget and Live Activity read this through an App Group on the same device. Nothing is sent to White Oak Labs or synced to iCloud.

Calendar (optional, read on your device)

If you tap "Connect calendar", SunSeek asks for calendar access so the Meeting Gap Scanner can find free time between meetings. It reads only the start and end times and busy/free availability of upcoming events. It never reads, stores or sends event titles, attendees, notes or locations. All-day and free events are skipped. Busy times are processed on your device; a short-lived copy of the busy intervals (kept for a few hours) is shared with SunSeek's widget through the App Group on the same device so it can update the plan. Busy times are never sent to White Oak Labs or anyone else, and SunSeek does not change your calendar. "Reserve my break" opens Apple's standard event editor filled in with the break; the event is added only if you save it. You can turn calendar access off at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars, and SunSeek then uses the free-time windows you set.

Sent to Apple to answer a request

  • Weather. When a place is set, its coordinate (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to Apple WeatherKit for the forecast and weather alerts. Forecasts are cached briefly on the device.
  • City search. What you type in city search goes to Apple Maps search.

Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions (all optional)

  • Calendar is used only after you tap "Connect calendar", as described above.
  • Location is used only when you tap "Use my location", never in the background. You can search for a city instead.
  • Notifications are off until you turn on reminders. They are scheduled on the device.
  • Camera and photos are used only when you add a sky photo. SunSeek keeps its own copy in the app.

Export and delete

Settings → Your data exports your data as JSON and CSV files through the share sheet. "Delete all SunSeek data" removes everything SunSeek stored on the device. Both are free. Deleting the app also removes its data. An optional diagnostics log (off by default) records event names only, stays on the device and is deleted when you turn it off.

Subscriptions

SunSeek Premium is bought through Apple. SunSeek never sees your payment details; it checks your subscription status with StoreKit on the device. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Feedback, children and advice

Feedback opens an email you choose to send; we use it only to reply. SunSeek is not directed at children under 13. It is a planning tool, not medical advice; weather and UV tips are general guidance.
