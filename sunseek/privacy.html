SunSeek by White Oak Labs

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

SunSeek: Light & Sleep ("SunSeek") is made by William Selaj (White Oak Labs). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. SunSeek has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. Your schedule stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your wake time, bedtime, sunlight check-ins, sleep and energy ratings, streak, settings, and accent color are stored on your iPhone. The Home Screen widget and Morning Live Activity read that same on-device schedule. Nothing in that schedule is sent to us.

Location

If you allow location access, SunSeek uses your coordinates once, on your device, to compute sunrise and sunset for your light plan. Those coordinates stay on your iPhone. We do not receive them. If you decline location, the app uses a Fort Lauderdale fallback and still works.

Notifications

Morning and evening reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to us.

Feedback

If you email us from the app or about TestFlight, we receive only what you send and use it only to reply to you.

Children

SunSeek is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove the schedule, check-ins, and settings stored on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
