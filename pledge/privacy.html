Pledge by White Oak Labs

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Pledge helps you keep your goals by putting money on the line for a charity. In short: your pledges stay on your phone, we have no accounts, we never sell data, and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Your pledges. Your goals, amounts, chosen charities, and check-ins are stored only on your device. We don't have a server and can't see them.
  • Purchases. If you subscribe to Pledge Pro, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active.

Donations

When you donate, Pledge opens a secure page from Every.org or the charity's own website. Your payment and contact details go directly to them, under their privacy policies. Pledge never receives, holds, or processes donation money.

Tracking

We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of your pledges. Cancel Pledge Pro anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

Pledge isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.
