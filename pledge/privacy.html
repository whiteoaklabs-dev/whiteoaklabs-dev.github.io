Privacy Policy

Pledge: Miss It, Charity Wins · Last updated November 13, 2026

Pledge helps you keep your goals with honest check-ins and a charity you choose, with an optional donation if you miss. In short: your pledges stay on your phone, we have no accounts, we never sell data, and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Your pledges. Your goals, amounts, chosen charities, check-ins and any donations you record are stored only on your device and shared with Pledge's own widgets. We don't have a server and can't see them.
  • Purchases. If you subscribe to Pledge Pro, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active.

Donations

If you choose to donate, Pledge opens Every.org or the charity's own website. Your payment and contact details go directly to them, under their privacy policies. Pledge never collects, holds, or processes donation money and can't see whether you donated.

Tracking

We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of your pledges. You can cancel Pledge Pro anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

Pledge isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.
