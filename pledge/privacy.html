Privacy Policy

Pledge: Miss It, Charity Wins · Last updated November 23, 2026

Pledge helps you keep your goals with honest check-ins and a charity you choose, with an optional donation if you miss. In short: your pledges stay on your phone, we have no accounts, we never sell data, and we don't show ads.

Data and storage

Pledge stores your goals, amounts, chosen charities, check-ins, self-reported donation records and settings on your device. Pledge widgets use shared local files to show your progress and record check-ins. Pledge has no account or server for this data. Apple processes Pledge Pro payments. Pledge uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to unlock and restore Pro. Your payment details are handled by Apple.

Donations

Opening Every.org sends the selected charity and suggested amount to that website. The charity website option opens its own donation page. Each site handles any details you enter under its own privacy policy. Pledge never collects, holds or processes donation payments. Your self-reported donation record stays in Pledge unless you delete it.

Sharing a kept pledge

When you tap Share, the iOS share sheet lets you send a card that can include your goal title and kept result. The destination you choose handles the card under its own policies.

Tracking

We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Deleting your data

Delete an individual pledge from its detail screen or delete the app to remove Pledge's local data. Your device backups are managed separately in Apple settings and may retain earlier copies. Turn off reminders in Pledge Settings or in your device notification settings. Manage or cancel Pledge Pro in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Children

Pledge isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email williamfastship@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents you send. We use those details to respond and retain them as needed to handle your request. To request deletion of your support correspondence, email us at the same address.
