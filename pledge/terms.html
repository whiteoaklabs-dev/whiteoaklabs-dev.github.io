How Pledge works

You set a goal, choose an amount and a charity, and check in on the honor system. Pledge is a personal commitment tool. It never charges you for a missed goal. Donating is optional: if you choose to give, you do it yourself on Every.org or the charity's own website.

Donations

Donations are between you and the charity or Every.org. White Oak Labs doesn't collect, hold or receive any part of them and isn't responsible for refunds, receipts, or tax treatment. Ask the charity or Every.org about those. Donations you record in Pledge are self-reported and not verified. Pledge isn't affiliated with Every.org or the listed charities.

Pledge Pro

One active pledge at a time is always free. Pledge Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription that adds unlimited active pledges and goal insights. Monthly and yearly plans are available; the App Store shows the price for your country or region in the app before you subscribe. There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your Apple ID is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. A Pledge Pro subscription pays only for app features; it doesn't fund or process donations.
