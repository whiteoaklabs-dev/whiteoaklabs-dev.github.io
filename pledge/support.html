Support

Pledge: Habit & Goal Tracker

Need help? Email williamfastship@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two. You can also tap Contact support in Pledge's Settings.

Checking in

Tap Mark done on a day you kept your goal. You can answer once a day for each pledge; a Day pledge takes one check-in within 24 hours of making it. Pledge runs on the honor system: there are no photos or proof.

Undo

Tapped the wrong answer? Undo clears it the same day, while the pledge is still running. An answer that ends a pledge can't be undone.

Not today and pace

On goals with rest days, like 3 days a week, the second answer is Not today. It logs a day you skipped, not a failure. On every-day goals and Day pledges it's Mark missed.

Week, month and year pledges show your pace, like "Need 2 more · 3 days to spare": how many check-ins you still need and how many days can pass without one. If skipping today would make the goal impossible, Pledge asks you to confirm before ending the pledge as missed.

Money and donations

Pledge never charges you for a missed goal and never receives, holds or processes donation money. If you miss, Pledge opens the charity's page on Every.org or the charity's own website, and you choose whether to donate there. Donations you mark in Pledge are self-reported: Pledge can't see or verify them. Pledge isn't affiliated with Every.org or the listed charities.

Pledge Pro

Your first pledge is free (one active pledge at a time). Pledge Pro unlocks unlimited pledges and goal insights for $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, with a 1-week free trial on the yearly plan.

  • Manage or cancel: on your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pledge, or in Pledge tap Settings → Pledge Pro → Manage. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.
  • Restore Purchases: in Pledge, open Settings → Upgrade to Pledge Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.

Your data

There's no account. Your pledges stay on your iPhone, and deleting the app removes them.
