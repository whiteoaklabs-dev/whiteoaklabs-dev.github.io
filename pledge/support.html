Support

Pledge: Miss It, Charity Wins

Need help? Email williamfastship@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two. You can also tap Contact support in Pledge's Settings.

Starting a pledge

Tap Create your first pledge (or +), write your goal, pick a timeframe, choose an amount and a charity, then tap Start pledge. Starting a pledge doesn't charge you anything.

Checking in

Tap Mark done on a day you kept your goal. You can answer once a day for each pledge; a Day pledge takes one check-in within 24 hours of making it. Pledge runs on the honor system: there are no photos or proof.

Undo

Tapped the wrong answer? Undo clears it the same day, while the pledge is still running. An answer that ends a pledge can't be undone, and Pledge asks you to confirm first.

Not today and pace

On goals with rest days, like 3 days a week, the second answer is Not today. It logs a day off, not a failure. On every-day goals and Day pledges it's Mark missed.

Week, month and year pledges show your pace, like "Need 2 more · 3 days to spare": how many check-ins you still need and how many days can pass without one. If skipping today would make the goal impossible, Pledge asks you to confirm before ending the pledge as missed.

Donations are optional

Pledge never charges you for a missed goal and never collects, holds or processes donation money. If you miss your target, you can tap Open donation page (the charity's page on Every.org) or Charity website and choose whether to donate there. I donated only records what you tell Pledge; Pledge can't see or verify donations, and you can undo the record. Pledge isn't affiliated with Every.org or the listed charities.

Widgets

Open Settings → Widgets & setup for previews and steps to add Pledge to your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Home Screen widgets can mark a pledge done without opening the app.

Pledge Pro

One active pledge at a time is always free. Pledge Pro adds unlimited active pledges and goal insights, with monthly and yearly subscriptions. The App Store shows the price for your country or region in the app before you subscribe. There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase, and subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

  • Manage or cancel: on your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pledge, or in Pledge tap Settings → Pledge Pro → Manage.
  • Restore Purchases: in Pledge, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases (also on the Pledge Pro screen), signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.

Your data

There's no account. Your pledges stay on your iPhone (shared only with Pledge's own widgets), and deleting the app removes them.
