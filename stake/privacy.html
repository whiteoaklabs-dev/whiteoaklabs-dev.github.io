Stake by White Oak Labs

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Stake helps you and your friends keep goals by putting a small amount of money on the line. This page explains what information Stake uses and why. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Account info. When you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we receive your name and email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we get a private relay address instead.
  • Your challenges. The goals you create, the friends you add, your daily check-ins (Done or Missed), notes, and the amounts owed between you and your friends.
  • Check-in photos. Photos you take for a check-in are stored on your phone.
  • Purchases. If you subscribe to Stake Pro, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card details. We only learn whether your subscription is active.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your challenges in sync across your devices and with the friends you share them with.
  • To send reminders you've turned on.
  • To unlock Stake Pro features if you subscribe.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who we share it with

  • Friends you challenge can see your name, your shared challenges, your check-ins, and your balance with them.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (secure database and sign-in), Apple (Sign in with Apple, payments, notifications), and Google (Sign in with Google). They handle data only to provide their service.
  • We may share information if required by law.

We never sell your personal information.

Money between friends

Stake only keeps track of who owes whom. It doesn't move, hold, or process money between friends. You settle up with each other however you like.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and your cloud data. Photos on your phone are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Stake isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and protected so only you and the friends you share challenges with can see it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Big changes will be shown in the app.
