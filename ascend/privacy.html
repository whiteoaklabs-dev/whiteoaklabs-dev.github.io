Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Ascend: Build Better Habits ("Ascend") is made by William Selaj (White Oak Labs). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. Ascend has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on your device

Your name (if you add one), your setup answers, areas, missions, progress, levels, streaks, challenges, and settings are stored on your iPhone.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Ascend syncs your data to your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit so it can appear on your other devices. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple ID. We cannot see or access it. You can turn this off in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Screen time

Ascend does not read your phone's Screen Time data. Any screen time limits or numbers you enter are typed in by you and stored like the rest of your data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to us.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple only tells the app whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Feedback

Feedback you write in the app is saved on your device and in your own iCloud. If you email us, we receive only what you send and use it only to reply to you.

Children

Ascend is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time with Reset in the app's Settings, or by deleting the app. Data in your iCloud account can be removed from your iPhone's iCloud settings under Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
