Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Link Later: Save for Later ("Link Later") is made by William Selaj (White Oak Labs). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app and its Safari and Chrome extensions.

The short version

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. Link Later has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. The links you save stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Saved links, titles, notes, tags, and settings are stored on your iPhone or iPad (and in your browser's local storage for the extensions). The share extension writes to the same on-device store. Nothing is sent to us.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. If you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple provides is stored on your device only. You can continue as a guest.

Face ID

If you turn on Face ID lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS. We never receive biometric data.

Feedback

If you email us from the app or about TestFlight, we receive only what you send and use it only to reply to you.

Children

Link Later is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Account → Delete account, or delete the app or extension, to remove everything stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
