Rivals Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026. Rivals is made by White Oak Labs.

Rivals is a contest between friends. There is no money betting. Apple Health supplies the score so a person does not type it.

What we store

  • Your Apple ID sign-in identifier (or the email address you use for a sign-in code), the name you choose, and a friend code.
  • Friend connections you create, and the challenges and leagues you join.
  • For a handicap challenge (Pro), one baseline number per person: your average daily total for that metric over the previous 14 or 28 days.
  • Daily totals for the metric of a challenge you joined (steps, workouts, active calories, miles, or a Pro metric). We store the total, not the raw Health samples.
  • Emoji reactions, wins and losses, and a push token if you allow notifications.
  • If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment. Our server checks Apple's signed transaction and stores your Rivals Pro status (plan, expiry and transaction ID) so Pro features work across your devices. We do not receive your card number.

Health

Health data stays on your iPhone until the app uploads a daily total for a challenge you joined. Rivals does not write to Apple Health. If you deny Health access, those days show as unsynced. We do not fill them with zero.

Who can see it

People in a challenge can see the totals, names, and reactions for that challenge. Friends can see your name, friend code, and win-loss record. We do not sell this data.

Where it lives

Accounts and challenges are stored in Supabase. Sign-in uses Sign in with Apple. You can delete your Rivals account in the app. If that same login is also used by another White Oak Labs app, deleting Rivals does not delete that other app’s data, and the login stays.
