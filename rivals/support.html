Common questions

How is the score kept?

Rivals reads daily totals for your challenge metric from Apple Health on your iPhone. Nobody types in a number. Open the app to sync your latest totals.

My score looks low or shows "unsynced".

Check that Rivals has permission for that metric in the Health app (Profile → Apps → Rivals) and open Rivals to sync. If Health access is off for a day, that day shows as unsynced rather than zero.

How do I invite someone?

Create a challenge, then share it by iMessage, WhatsApp or a link, or give them your challenge code. They need Rivals on an iPhone with Apple Health.

What does Rivals Pro add?

Free includes unlimited 1-on-1 challenges with steps, workouts, active calories and miles. Rivals Pro adds every Health metric, groups and season leagues of up to 10 people, handicap challenges scored against each person's own baseline, and Rivals Rewind recaps. Pro is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a free week for new subscribers.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a purchase, open Rivals, go to the You tab and tap Restore purchases.

How do I delete my account?

In Rivals, open the You tab and tap Delete account. This removes your Rivals profile, friends and challenge data.
