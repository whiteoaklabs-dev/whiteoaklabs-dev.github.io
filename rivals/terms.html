Rivals Terms of Use

Last updated October 13, 2026. Rivals is made by White Oak Labs.

Rivals lets friends run a timed contest of Apple Health activity. It is not gambling. No money is staked on a result.

The free app

You can run unlimited one-on-one challenges of steps, workouts, active calories, or miles, for 3, 7, or 14 days.

Rivals Pro

Rivals Pro is an auto-renewable subscription: $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year, each with a 7-day free trial. Pro adds group leagues of up to 10 people, season stats, and the custom metrics flights, exercise minutes, and cycling miles.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. A subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription, where Apple requires that.

Scores

The app reads Apple Health on your iPhone and sends the daily total. Do not try to alter the score. A modified client could post a number; that is outside the app we ship, and we may remove those results.

Accounts

You need Sign in with Apple. You can delete the Rivals account from the You tab. If you share that login with another White Oak Labs app, that other app’s data is left in place.
